# Conjectures

## Active

### C-1: Multi-session seed campaigns are reliable
- **Statement**: Up to six fresh producer sessions sharing only the current clean-verified candidate—not prior events or any rendered history—will yield at least four valid numeric events, two distinct candidates, two distinct scores, positive progress, and remaining headroom in at least 80% of prequalified task-model campaigns.
- **Kill criterion**: Fewer than four of the first five treatment-blind qualification campaigns pass, or any campaign requires treatment-specific prompts, renderer access, unmetered feedback, or post-hoc event selection.
- **Confidence**: 70
- **Evidence for**: Dense histories arose when agents iterated; the evaluator and exact-candidate path now pass.
- **Evidence against**: Kimi h2-v2 ended after inspection despite explicit eligibility wording.
- **Experiments pending**: Minimal paid campaigns on unused task-model pairs after the frozen route and task gates.
- **Status**: ACTIVE

### C-2: Structured organization improves verified progress efficiency
- **Statement**: At least one of R1 or R2 improves paired mean normalized outer-verified progress-AUC over R0 by at least 0.25 meaningful-gain units without final-score inferiority.
- **Kill criterion**: In an adequately powered frozen design, both structured arms' simultaneous upper intervals are below 0.25, or effects fail the frozen cross-model consistency rule.
- **Confidence**: 35
- **Evidence for**: One earlier uncontrolled run favored a state brief 7/8 versus 4/8.
- **Evidence against**: Raw/flat/brief comparisons had no reliable winner; no randomized treatment evidence exists.
- **Experiments pending**: Eligible histories, blinded randomized pilot, gated replication.
- **Status**: ACTIVE
