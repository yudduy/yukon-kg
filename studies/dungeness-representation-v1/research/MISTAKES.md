# Mistakes

## M-1: Single-session history assumption
- **What happened**: A healthy Kimi producer ended after inspection with zero evaluations.
- **Why**: Seed eligibility was instructional, not an orchestration invariant.
- **Pattern**: Agent compliance was treated as apparatus reliability.
- **Fix**: Make the seed a bounded multi-session campaign and classify each session without hiding failures.

## M-2: Task screening consumed the first pilot slot serially
- **What happened**: Several tasks were discovered to be saturated or history-sparse only after paid seed attempts.
- **Why**: Model-free verifier stability was checked, but producer-history density and remaining headroom were not jointly prequalified.
- **Pattern**: A necessary gate was tested too late.
- **Fix**: Freeze a broader reserve and require treatment-blind campaign qualification before randomization.
