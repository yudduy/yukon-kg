# Mistakes and Corrections

- **Saying “task-dependent” too strongly.** The pilot shows heterogeneity, not a validated mapping from task to representation. Correction: say “research-state-dependent hypothesis.”
- **Treating exact block winners as labels.** With one realization per arm and wide uncertainty, an exact winner may be noise. Correction: use practical best sets and require replicated labels.
- **Assuming view access means view use.** All VLIW sessions opened the view, but none cited a concrete prior score. Correction: score grounded decisions and event-ID use.
- **Missing a sensitivity control.** The pilot could not show that the treatment was strong enough to alter behavior. Correction: add a positive-control decision probe before full chains.
- **Underweighting actionability.** History structure alone cannot distinguish Toy from VLIW here. Correction: add treatment-blind features describing feasible next experiments, headroom, noise, and source-derived solution strength.
- **Inferring mechanism from VLIW outcomes.** VLIW rounds had zero development evaluations and failed sessions; terminal artifacts determined outcomes. Correction: preserve outcome validity while declining a causal reasoning claim.
- **Exposing unrestricted Kimi reasoning inside a short answer allowance.** Probe v1 could not distinguish representation use from whether reasoning finished before JSON. Correction: fail the sensitivity gate, exclude v1, and calibrate transport separately.
- **Assuming excluded reasoning guarantees final content.** Probe v2 still produced malformed/no-text Kimi responses and exhausted a frozen retry cell. Correction: preserve v2 as an administrative exclusion and retain low-effort reasoning as a parseable fallback in the final version.
- **Treating a plausible routing table as an empirical classification.** The sensitivity-valid v3 probe found R1 tied R0 at higher cost and R2 made the only target error. Correction: reject the router and use R0 throughout the tested domain.
