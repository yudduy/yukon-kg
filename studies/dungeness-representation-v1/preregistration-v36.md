# Dungeness Representation Pilot v36 Preregistration

## Status

This amendment is frozen before any v36 model call. All v34 and v35 treatment
cells are excluded from scientific analysis. After observing v35 execution, an
apparatus audit found that the milestone selector used only development
evaluator captures and could omit the final candidate left by a research
session. The defect affected outcome measurement, so no affected cell is
salvaged. v36 restarts all six blocks with fresh opaque identities.

## Fixed scientific design

v36 retains the frozen six histories, R0/R1/R2 views, treatment orders,
GPT-5.6 Sol consumer, identical prompts and controls, three fresh sessions,
and the $9/600,000-token/90-minute/12-evaluation chain caps. The only scientific
apparatus change is faithful outer-milestone capture.

At the end of every round, Dungeness seals the exact terminal candidate from
trusted Harbor storage into a content-addressed, read-only record. Development
evaluations and terminal candidates are placed on one cumulative chain-budget
timeline. The 0%, 25%, 50%, 75%, and 100% milestones select the most recent
candidate available at or before each fraction; 100% selects the final observed
candidate. Every selected artifact is verified only after the chain using the
sealed outer evaluator. Invalid artifacts remain treatment outcomes and the
analysis applies the preregistered last-valid carry rule.

Terminal candidates do not enter handoffs or later research views unless they
were separately submitted through the metered development evaluator and then
clean-reverified as the best handoff candidate. Outer scores remain sealed
until the three-round chain ends.

## Exclusion and budget policy

- v34 is apparatus-only because the selector defect affected all cells.
- v35 is apparatus-only because the defect was discovered after one complete
  chain and one interrupted chain had exposed outcomes.
- v36 uses one deterministic replacement for each of the original 18 v34
  cells. Experimental fields other than opaque identity and study label are
  byte-equivalent to the frozen v34 source.
- All prior charges and fail-closed reservations remain in the shared ledger.
  The absolute study ceiling remains $500 and confirmation remains impossible
  without a separate user-approved go decision.

## Mandatory release gates

- full Dungeness, gateway, and Yukon suites pass;
- terminal candidates from native and external runs are sealed, immutable,
  content-addressed, and reproducible under clean outer verification;
- the selector reproduces an excluded real v35 chain's 1,543-cycle terminal
  candidate instead of silently carrying its 7,337-cycle baseline;
- all 18 replacement links are one-to-one, opaque, balanced 6/6/6, and form
  six complete randomized blocks;
- every candidate, view, task, checkpoint, model, prompt, and budget field
  matches the frozen v34 source;
- source hashes, implementation hashes, manifests, and run specs are captured
  in a new freeze with independent validation;
- live provider routing, Docker access, model-free preflight, and zero running
  experiment containers pass immediately before launch.

Any failed gate stops v36 before treatment. The paid run is fail-fast: an
administrative failure stops scheduling until classified and linked.
