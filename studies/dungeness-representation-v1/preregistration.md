## Experimental unit and assignment

The unit is one complete three-round post-fork research trajectory. A block is task × frozen history × consumer model. The three arms are assigned anonymous run IDs and one of six balanced execution orders using `sha256-sort-v1` and the seed in `protocol.yaml`. Internal evaluations are repeated measurements, not independent samples.

A seed history is eligible only when its producer used no research view, shares the frozen task, checkpoint, producer identity, and study budget scope, and contains at least four distinct valid numeric candidate evaluations. Dungeness deterministically selects and clean-verifies the best candidate before exporting trusted events and compiling R0/R1/R2. Seed records become immutable once randomized assignments exist.

## Outcomes

For task \(t\), one meaningful gain is 10% of its direction-corrected official baseline-to-reference gap. Progress itself starts at the clean outer-verified score of the candidate given to every arm; the official baseline is never substituted for that score. At each fixed budget fraction, preserve the most recent captured candidate at or before the first exhausted cumulative cap among USD, tokens, elapsed time, and development evaluations. If no new valid candidate exists, carry the last valid artifact. The sealed outer verifier scores those artifacts only after the chain. The primary outcome is trapezoidal AUC of starting-candidate-relative improvement divided by meaningful gain. Regressions and reference-beating gains remain unclipped.

Secondary outcomes are final outer score, time and cost to first meaningful gain, valid-evaluation rate, repeated experiments, representation-reading cost, and candidate diversity. They are descriptive in the pilot.

## Pilot decision

The first-model pilot contains six blocks and 18 chains. Replicate with Kimi K3 only if at least one structured arm:

1. has paired mean progress-AUC improvement of at least 0.25;
2. wins at least four of six blocks;
3. is final-score noninferior within 0.25 units; and
4. has no apparatus or provenance violation.

Retain R0 if both structured arms' upper intervals are below 0.25. Treat intervals crossing 0.25 as inconclusive. A model- or task-direction reversal rejects a universal winner and motivates a separately tested conditional policy.

## Missingness and exclusions

Agent crashes, invalid patches, treatment-induced timeouts, exhausted budgets, and failed verification remain intention-to-treat outcomes; carry the last valid artifact forward. A local hard-cap stop is expected execution, not a provider outage: the adapter restores the saved best candidate and allows clean verification. An administrative failure may be declared only from infrastructure metadata before unblinding. Repeat the entire block under a linked replacement ID and preserve the failed attempt. Renderer or access bugs invalidate the renderer version and require unused histories.

All three rounds share one $9, 600,000-token, 90-minute, 12-evaluation ledger; round caps are $3, 200,000 tokens, 30 minutes, and four evaluations. The gateway atomically reserves conservative input plus the forwarded output maximum against both token and frozen-price USD budgets before recording the prompt or contacting OpenRouter, clamps output limits, and fails closed when a model price tier is unknown. Before Harbor starts, an atomic host ledger reserves every seed or treatment run's full cap against the shared $500 ceiling. Unknown labeled scopes fail closed; missing usage or in-flight cost retains the full reservation; persisted cost rounds upward. Paid Farmshare dispatch is disabled until remote and local runs can share that same atomic authority.
