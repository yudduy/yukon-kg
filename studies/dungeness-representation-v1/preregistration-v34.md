- all full Dungeness, gateway, and Yukon suites pass;
- malformed, path-escaping, duplicate, or byte-inconsistent payload manifests
  fail before projection;
- two compilations of every history are byte-identical and every arm receives
  identical atom bytes exactly once;
- every initial view accepts a task-matched, clean-verified or real Dungeness
  event export after its cutoff and re-renders under 32k total/4k index tokens;
- a repeated-event stress projection demonstrates sufficient space for the
  full 12-event chain without dropping exact diffs or semantic facts;
- native and both external task-family Docker smokes reproduce sealed starts;
- assignments use all six treatment permutations once and leak no labels;
- a new content-addressed freeze independently revalidates, the live route and
  pricing remain pinned, and active spend reservations equal zero.

Any gate failure stops v34 before treatment. Post-contact apparatus failure
excludes the affected version in full and rotates identities again.
